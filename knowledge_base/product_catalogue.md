# VoxSell Enterprise AI Suite - Product Catalogue

## Overview
VoxSell is an autonomous, real-time AI sales voice agent engineered for enterprise sales, lead qualification, and instantaneous inbound enquiry management.

## Products & Features

### 1. VoxSell Voice Core
- **Description**: Real-time ultra-low latency voice conversational engine (<1.5s voice-to-voice turnaround).
- **Supported Languages**: English, Hindi, Punjabi with automatic code-switching and dynamic accent adaptation.
- **Audio Codecs**: 16kHz PCM, OPUS, μ-law/a-law telephony standards.

### 2. VoxSell Smart BANT Qualifier
- **Description**: Conversational qualification framework mapping Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline.
- **Scoring**: Algorithmic 0–100 lead score calculation updated continuously across dialogue turns.
- **Sentiment Engine**: Sub-sentence acoustic and semantic sentiment monitoring with objection detection.

### 3. VoxSell Autonomous Closer
- **Description**: Direct calendar integration for zero-friction appointment booking.
- **Integrations**: Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, Cal.com.
- **CRM Sync**: Real-time bi-directional sync with HubSpot, Salesforce, and PostgreSQL native CRM.
